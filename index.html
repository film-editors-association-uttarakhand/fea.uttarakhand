The Masterminds

The Architects of FEA Uttarakhand

We often see the final cinematic masterpiece, but rarely the architects who bleed in the shadows to build it.

The Film Editor's Association did not just happen. It was forged through late nights, relentless dedication, and the pure passion of these legendary creators who united a fragmented industry.

Without their unmatched technical mastery and profound love for the craft, this 19,000+ peoples strong empire would simply not exist. They are the ultimate Masterminds/Stategist/Guardian of the FEA.

Visionary & The Ultimate of 2024, 2025 & 2026

Executive Director

Sarvesh Kushwaha

The visionary narrative architect setting global standards for regional cinema. He seamlessly blends complex VFX with deeply emotional storytelling. His mastery over the timeline elevates every cut into a cinematic masterpiece. A true pioneer shaping the future of Uttarakhand's post-production.
Design Core

Creative Director

Shivani Joshi
